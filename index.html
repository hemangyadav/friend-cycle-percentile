Report sentence

Method: the calculator uses the published age-decade, sex-specific cycle-ergometer values from Table 3. Between the 10th and 90th percentile thresholds, it estimates a percentile by linear interpolation between adjacent deciles. It reports values outside those bounds as below the 10th or above the 90th percentile rather than extrapolating.

Reference: Kaminsky LA, Arena R, Myers J, et al. Updated Reference Standards for Cardiorespiratory Fitness Measured with Cardiopulmonary Exercise Testing: Data from FRIEND. Mayo Clinic Proceedings. 2022;97(2):285–293. DOI: 10.1016/j.mayocp.2021.08.020.
